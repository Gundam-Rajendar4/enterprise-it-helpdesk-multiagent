Hardware Troubleshooting Knowledge Base

Monitor not turning on:
1. Confirm power cable is connected at both monitor and wall outlet
2. Confirm the outlet itself works (test with another device)
3. Check the monitor's power button was actually pressed
4. Try a different video cable (HDMI/DisplayPort) if monitor powers on
   but shows no signal
5. If monitor still fails, submit a hardware replacement request via
   the IT Asset Portal

Laptop not charging:
1. Check charger cable and adapter for visible damage
2. Try a different power outlet
3. Restart the laptop
4. If issue persists, this may indicate battery or charging port failure
   - escalate to Hardware team
