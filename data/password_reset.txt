Password Reset Knowledge Base

To reset a forgotten password:
1. Go to the company self-service portal at portal.company.com/reset
2. Enter your registered work email address
3. Click "Send Reset Link" - a link will be emailed within 5 minutes
4. The reset link expires after 30 minutes
5. If no email arrives, check spam folder before escalating to IT

Note: Never share passwords over email, chat, or phone. IT staff will
never ask for your current password.

Account lockout: After 5 failed login attempts, accounts lock for 15
minutes automatically. Do not attempt repeated logins during lockout.
